Chiropractic licensees, Contra Costa County

Every Board of Chiropractic Examiners license with a Contra Costa address of record (DCA license search, pulled 2026-10-26), placed on the County Assessor parcel.
Click a point for the record. Points on one parcel are fanned out so each can be clicked. Base map: OpenFreeMap (OpenMapTiles schema, OpenStreetMap data). County parcel lines and aerial: Contra Costa County GIS (gis.cccounty.us). Parcel land use: County Assessor use code (34 = Medical, Dental; 33 = Office Buildings; 11 = single family home).
